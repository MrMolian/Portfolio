# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective collaborators, clients, and investors discovering Moaad Barkache. They need a fast, memorable introduction to who he is and a clear route to his active venture and professional profiles.

## Product Purpose

Present Moaad's personal portfolio as the public home for his work as a Belgian tech entrepreneur, OsClay co-founder, and physicist. Success is a visitor leaving with a strong sense of ambition and knowing where to continue the conversation.

## Brand Personality

Quietly ambitious, exacting, and cinematic. The site should feel like an atelier for ideas: generous breathing room, impeccable material detail, and a sense that each project is being shaped with care.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup dashboards, template portfolio grids, loud tech visual clichés, and imitation luxury branding. Do not make the site feel youthful in a casual or gimmicky way.

## Design Principles

- Make the first impression atmospheric, then make the information easy to find.
- Let composition, restraint, and material detail create confidence.
- Use contrast and scale to express ambition without overstating claims.
- Keep paths to people and projects frictionless.
- Treat each project as a chapter with room to breathe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, work across modern screen sizes, maintain visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
